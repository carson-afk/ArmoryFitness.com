What Sets Our Program Apart

Program Description

Armory Fitness brings Hyrox training to Ferndale. Our program is purpose-built to prepare you for race day. Every session mirrors the demands of the actual event: sustained aerobic output paired with heavy, skill-based functional movements that challenge your entire body. Sled pushes and pulls, ski ergs, rowing, burpee broad jumps, sandbag lunges, wall balls, and farmers carries, all coached with precision so you move efficiently and stay healthy.

Race-Ready Programming

Every workout focuses on specific demands of a Hyrox race. Over the course of your training program, you will train the exact eight workout stations alongside plenty of running volume so nothing surprises you on race day.

Expert Functional Coaching

Our coaches break down each movement, from sled and ski erg to sandbag lunges, wall balls, and more, so you build efficiency and power. Proper form under fatigue is the foundation of a faster finish.

Scalable for Every Level

Whether you are a first-time competitor or a seasoned racer, workouts scale to meet you where you are. Progress is built week over week so you arrive at the start line stronger each time.

Community & Accountability

Train alongside fellow athletes who share your goals. The energy of a group chasing the same race keeps motivation high. Join your fellow athletes for weekly training at Armory Fitness.
