Why Lynden trains here

The closest 24-hour gym with real coaching.

Lynden is a tight-knit Dutch-heritage town in the heart of the Nooksack Valley. From here it’s 12 minutes east on Highway 539 to our Main Street location in Ferndale. Lynden is one of our top three feeder communities — the drive becomes part of your routine.

  • 24-hour keycard access — train at 5 a.m., 11 p.m., or any hour in between
  • Full strength platform with racks, bumpers, dumbbells to 100 lbs
  • Cardio deck, free weights, recovery wing (cold plunge + infrared sauna + red light)
  • Group fitness classes — spin, HIIT, strength, yoga across the week
  • ACE-certified personal trainers if you want a coach
  • From $43/mo for the basic, $75 for unlimited classes, family + couples rates available
